# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-24

- Scan receipts with the camera, gallery or PDF imports; on-device OCR with Spanish supermarket templates
- Correct anything the scan got wrong — store, date, items, total — before and after saving
- Confidential: everything stays on the device; no accounts, no cloud, AI refinement runs on-device
- Analytics by category, store and time; history with search and filters
- JSON backup via the share sheet
- Accessibility, i18n (EN/ES), dark mode and haptic feedback
- Local-only crash log surfaced in Settings → Diagnostics
